Question 1 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
Three numbers are in the ratio of 3 : 4 : 5 and their L.C.M. is 2400. Their H.C.F. is:
A 40 ✓ Correct Answer
B 80
C 120
D 200
Step-by-Step Solution: Let the numbers be 3x, 4x, and 5x. Their LCM is 60x = 2400 => x = 40. The HCF of 3x, 4x, 5x is x, which is 40.
Question 2 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
The ratio of two numbers is 3 : 4 and their H.C.F. is 4. Their L.C.M. is:
A 12
B 16
C 24
D 48 ✓ Correct Answer
Step-by-Step Solution: The numbers are 3 * 4 = 12 and 4 * 4 = 16. LCM(12, 16) = 48.
Question 3 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
The L.C.M. of two numbers is 48. The numbers are in the ratio 2 : 3. Then the sum of the numbers is:
A 28
B 32
C 40 ✓ Correct Answer
D 64
Step-by-Step Solution: Let the numbers be 2x and 3x. Their LCM is 6x = 48 => x = 8. The numbers are 16 and 24. Sum = 16 + 24 = 40.
Question 4 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
If one-third of one-fourth of a number is 15, then three-tenth of that number is:
A 35
B 36
C 45
D 54 ✓ Correct Answer
Step-by-Step Solution: (1/3) * (1/4) * N = 15 => N / 12 = 15 => N = 180. Therefore, (3/10) * 180 = 54.
Question 5 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
Three times the first of three consecutive odd integers is 3 more than twice the third. The third integer is:
A 9
B 11
C 13
D 15 ✓ Correct Answer
Step-by-Step Solution: Let the integers be x, x + 2, x + 4. 3x = 2(x + 4) + 3 => 3x = 2x + 11 => x = 11. The third integer is 11 + 4 = 15.
Question 6 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
The difference between a two-digit number and the number obtained by interchanging the positions of its digits is 36. What is the difference between the two digits of that number?
A 3
B 4 ✓ Correct Answer
C 9
D Cannot be determined
Step-by-Step Solution: Difference = (10x + y) - (10y + x) = 9(x - y) = 36 => x - y = 4.
Question 7 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
A two-digit number is such that the product of the digits is 8. When 18 is added to the number, then the digits are reversed. The number is:
A 18
B 24 ✓ Correct Answer
C 42
D 81
Step-by-Step Solution: Let the number be 10x + y. xy = 8. Adding 18 reverses digits: 10x + y + 18 = 10y + x => 9(y - x) = 18 => y - x = 2. With xy = 8 and y - x = 2, we get x = 2, y = 4. The number is 24.
Question 8 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
If the number 481 * 673 is completely divisible by 9, then the smallest whole number in place of * will be:
A 2
B 5
C 6
D 7 ✓ Correct Answer
Step-by-Step Solution: Sum of digits = 4 + 8 + 1 + * + 6 + 7 + 3 = 29 + *. The nearest multiple of 9 greater than or equal to 29 is 36. Thus, * = 36 - 29 = 7.
Question 9 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
The value of a washing machine depreciates at the rate of 10% every year. If its present value is Rs. 8,748, then what was the price of the washing machine three years ago?

Question 14 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
A train travelled a certain distance at a uniform speed. Had the speed been 6 km/h more, it would have needed 4 hours less. Had the speed been 6 km/h less, it would have needed 6 hours more. The distance, in km, travelled by the train is:
A 620 km
B 720 km ✓ Correct Answer
C 540 km
D 840 km
Step-by-Step Solution: Using D = s*(s+6)*4/6 = s*(s-6)*6/6 => 4(s+6) = 6(s-6) => 4s + 24 = 6s - 36 => 2s = 60 => s = 30 km/h. Distance D = 30 * 36 * 4 / 6 = 720 km.
Question 15 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
A boat's speed in still water is 22 km/h, while the river is flowing with a speed of 8 km/h. The time taken to cover a certain distance upstream is 4 hours more than the time taken to cover the same distance downstream. Find the distance.
A 100 km
B 105 km ✓ Correct Answer
C 110 km
D 115 km
Step-by-Step Solution: Downstream speed = 22 + 8 = 30 km/h. Upstream speed = 22 - 8 = 14 km/h. D/14 - D/30 = 4 => D * (16 / 420) = 4 => D = 4 * 420 / 16 = 105 km.
Question 16 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
A boat goes 20 km upstream and 44 km downstream in 8 hours. In 5 hours, it goes 15 km upstream and 22 km downstream. Determine the speed of the boat in still water.
A 5 km/h
B 8 km/h ✓ Correct Answer
C 10 km/h
D 12 km/h
Step-by-Step Solution: Let 1/u = x and 1/v = y. 20x + 44y = 8 and 15x + 22y = 5 => 30x + 44y = 10. Subtracting gives 10x = 2 => x = 1/5 (upstream = 5 km/h) and y = 1/11 (downstream = 11 km/h). Speed in still water = (5 + 11)/2 = 8 km/h.
Question 17 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
A can do a work in 15 days and B in 20 days. If they work on it together for 4 days, then the fraction of the work that is left is:
A 5/7
B 8/15 ✓ Correct Answer
C 9/16
D 3/10
Step-by-Step Solution: A's 1-day work = 1/15, B's 1-day work = 1/20. Together in 1 day = 1/15 + 1/20 = 7/60. In 4 days = 4 * 7/60 = 7/15. Work left = 1 - 7/15 = 8/15.
Question 18 [+1 Mark, -1 Negative Mark]
A, B and C can do a piece of work in 20, 30 and 60 days respectively. In how many days can A do the work if he is assisted by B and C on every third day?
A 5 days

D 20 days
Step-by-Step Solution: Day 1 (A) = 1/20. Day 2 (A) = 1/20. Day 3 (A+B+C) = 1/20 + 1/30 + 1/60 = 6/60 = 1/10. Work in 3 days = 1/20 + 1/20 + 1/10 = 1/5. Entire work completes in 5 * 3 = 15 days.
